Three things to know

01

Draw & drag

Click or touch the canvas and drag to inject color into the fluid field. Change color mid-stroke and it blends physically where the paths cross.

02

Pick a tool & motion

The dock at the bottom holds art modes, motion physics (spin, zig-zag, ripple, dispersion), jelly bodies and lighting — hover to expand, like a macOS dock.

03

Play with sound

Pick a Lo-Fi track from the audio pod — the fluid's force, ripple intensity and neon glow all pulse in sync with its frequencies in real time.
